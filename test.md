## Capital Fabrication

This drive will be assembled as a single piece of equipment that meets the university's [definition of capital equipment](https://policy.umn.edu/finance/property#:~:text=The%20cost%20of%20the%20equipment,by%20one%20year%20or%20greater.):

- The cost of the equipment must be $5,000 or greater
- The University must have title or ownership rights of the equipment
- The equipment must have a useful life of one year or greater.

We asked the Controller's office to create a Fabrication ID for this

- **Fabrication ID:** `FAB202606002`

This fabrication ID is provided to purchasing when making any purchases associated with this fabrication.
